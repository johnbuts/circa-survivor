## Hedge

One ticket, not a round robin.

- **IND + TB + NYJ + MIA**
- Stake **~$246** (cover-sized: if all four picks die, profit replaces the $10k in fees)
- Circa decimal ~41.66, American about +4066

Do **not** also buy the cover-sized 3-legs. Those cost ~$2k cash and about $220 of extra EV, and they all hit in the same fire as the 4-leg.

No $10 round-robin sprinkle unless you want to feel busy. It does not move the mean.

## What you are buying

- If favorites hold, chip is ~$1,007 and a live book is worth about the fee back. Boring on purpose.
- If LAC dies and your four win, field drops to ~11.7k and ten live tickets mark ~**$17k**. That is the week-1 jackpot. Seating does that, not the parlay.
- If your four all lose, the 4-leg is built so week P&L on fees is ~**$0**, not −$10k.

## Why not two-team SEA / BAL

Slightly cleaner fade, worse week-1 product: ~14% wipe, ~$1,700 insurance, and it spends Christmas. Four non-holiday favorites is the better stack: cheap wipe (~$246), wipeout row covered, still almost none of the crowd.
